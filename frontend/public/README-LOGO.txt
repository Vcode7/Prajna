========================================================================
PRAJNA - COMPANY LOGO SWITCHING GUIDE
========================================================================

The application dynamically loads the active company logo from this folder.

ACTIVE LOGO FILES:
- logo.png  (or main.png / logo.jpg / main.jpg)

PRESERVED LOGOS:
- Numentrix:
    * logo-numentrix.png  (or numentrix.png)
    * logo-numentrix.jpg  (or numentrix.jpg)
- DataNex:
    * logo-datanex.png    (or datanex.png)
    * logo-datanex.jpg    (or datanex.jpg)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO SWITCH LOGOS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. To switch to NUMENTRIX:
   Copy or rename 'logo-numentrix.png' (or 'numentrix.png') to 'logo.png' (or 'main.png')

2. To switch to DATANEX:
   Copy or rename 'logo-datanex.png' (or 'datanex.png') to 'logo.png' (or 'main.png')

The app automatically recognizes both 'logo' and 'main' names, with .png or .jpg!
